One of the most common questions I hear from lot owners and residents is: "Where do our strata levies actually go?"

There is often confusion about who receives levy funds, how those funds are allocated, and who is responsible for managing them. Many people assume that strata levies are simply paid to the strata manager, but the reality is considerably more structured.

Levies are collected by the Owners Corporation and allocated across two key funding streams: the Administrative Fund, which supports day-to-day operations, and the Capital Works Fund, which provides for the long term maintenance and renewal of common property assets.

Another common misconception is that the Strata Committee is a paid body. In fact, strata committee members are volunteers who contribute their time and expertise to help oversee the financial management and decision-making processes of the Owners Corporation on behalf of all owners.

Understanding where levy contributions go is fundamental to understanding how a strata scheme operates, how assets are maintained, and how communities protect the value of their properties over the long term.
