Services

Strata & Residential Buildings

Expert advisory for strata managers, building owners and residential operators on smart building strategy, BMS, and technology-driven resident experience.

Overview

We help strata and residential building stakeholders apply practical technology strategy to improve asset value, minimise operational cost and enhance occupant experience. Services span assessments, roadmaps, BMS reviews, procurement support and delivery assurance.

Building Technology Assessments

Comprehensive technical and commercial assessment of current systems (BMS, access control, CCTV, network, HVAC controls, IoT sensors) including risk, technical debt and remediation options.

Smart Building Roadmaps

Tailored multi-year roadmaps aligned to asset management plans and tenant/resident needs — prioritising low-risk, high-value interventions that enable future innovation.

Building Management Systems (BMS) Reviews

Vendor-agnostic BMS reviews that focus on interoperability, cybersecurity hardening, controls rationalisation and performance baseline definition.

Digital Transformation & Resident Experience

Strategy for digital services, resident engagement platforms, operational automation and data governance to deliver measurable operational savings and improved satisfaction.

Fixed-Price Consulting Packages

We offer packaged assessments and roadmap engagements suitable for strata committees and small portfolios — clear scoping, fixed fees and executive deliverables.
