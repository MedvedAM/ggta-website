Smart Building Health Check

Independent review of building technology resilience, security, lifecycle exposure, and operational readiness across critical systems.

Overview

The Smart Building Health Check provides a practical and executive-ready assessment of the technology foundations that support modern residential and commercial buildings. It helps owners, operators, strata committees and facilities teams understand where risks, gaps and legacy decisions are creating operational, cyber, safety or financial exposure.

Review of:

  • Connectivity Infrastructure
  • Wi-Fi & Network Resilience
  • CCTV Systems
  • Access Control
  • Intercoms
  • OT/IT Segmentation
  • Cybersecurity
  • Vendor Lock-in Risks
  • Lifecycle Risks
  • Energy & Sustainability Technology

Deliverables

  • Executive Summary — concise overview of critical findings, business impact and strategic implications.
  • Risk Register — ranked issues covering operational, security, commercial and lifecycle exposure.
  • Maturity Assessment — evaluation of current capability, resilience and technology governance.
  • Prioritised Roadmap — staged recommendations aligned to cost, urgency and value.
  • Budget Guidance — indicative cost ranges and investment pathways to inform planning and procurement.
Request a Smart Building Health Check

Quick Facts

  • Typical clients: Strata communities, building owners, property managers, operators
  • Engagement: 2–6 weeks depending on asset scope and complexity
  • Output: Executive-ready assessment and action plan
Why this matters

Smart building systems increasingly drive resident experience, operational efficiency, safety and asset value. A structured health check helps identify high-risk gaps before they become costly failures or compliance issues.
